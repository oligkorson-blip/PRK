## 1. Research (summary)

Peers sell urban necessity and scarcity (Primevest), invite-only access (Curated Capital / Equigini / BridgeMarket), and clear process with honest risk (EstateGuru). Retail alts hype is rejected.

Parkwise: invite-only deal room for continental parking and mobility hub allocations. Catalogue is public to browse. Allocations follow human review.

---

## 2. Positioning

**Internal line:** Parkwise is an invite-only deal room for continental European parking and mobility hub allocations. Terms are published. Onboarding is handled by people. Capital is at risk.

**Not:** a fund, crowdfunding, a marketplace, or a custody bank.  
**Yes:** a private allocation workflow with a published catalogue and an operator-leased infrastructure thesis.

**Never say:** guaranteed, safe, monthly income assured, green yield, invest like the 1%, unverified AUM, funding progress bars.

---

## 3. Voice guide

### Warm professional (default)

Think European private bank website, not a startup landing page.

- Clear, polite, confident.
- Full sentences. Natural rhythm. No slogan stacking.
- Explain before you ask.
- Exclusivity is stated once, calmly (“Access is by invitation”). Not performed.
- Risk is stated plainly beside CTAs. Not dramatised. Not buried.

### Calm institutional (Why parking + Guides only)

- Slightly quieter. More thesis, less conversion pressure.
- Factual urban and infrastructure language.
- Soft CTAs at the end, not in every paragraph.

### Do not use

- Punchy hooks (“Serious about access? Say so.”)
- Club theatre (“Members only past this point.”)
- Confrontational risk lines (“If a page felt like a guarantee, leave.”)
- Em dashes
- Robotic compounds as headlines (“mobility infrastructure finance”)
- Fake warmth (“You’re going to love…”)

**FAQ**

**Kicker:** Questions  
**H2:** Common questions.

**Q: How does an allocation work?**  
You select a hub and an option from the minimum ticket, then register your interest. After KYC and confirmation by our team, a holding record is created. The operator manages the asset. Parkwise maintains your portal records. Your capital remains at risk.

**Q: Are target yields guaranteed?**  
No. Target yields are contractual targets agreed with the operator. Occupancy, pricing, and operating costs can change. You may receive less than the target, or lose part or all of your capital.

**Q: How often will I receive updates?**  
Reporting follows the documents for each allocation. Material updates that affect your records are shown in the portal when recorded by our team.

**Q: Can I exit early?**  
Parking allocations are generally illiquid. You should plan to hold for the stated term. Any secondary transfer depends on finding a buyer and may involve a discount. Please do not allocate funds you may need at short notice.

**Q: What fees does Parkwise charge?**  
Structuring and administration fees are shown in euros before you confirm. Where projected figures are presented net of fees, this is stated on the page.

### 4.3 Why parking *(calm institutional)*

**Kicker:** Why parking  
**H1:** Parking as urban infrastructure.  
**Lead:** Parking is a practical part of how cities move people and goods. Demand for well-located off-street capacity remains, even as policy reshapes the street.

1. **Location and scarcity**  
   Suitable off-street sites are limited. Replacing them nearby is often difficult.

2. **Pressure on the curb**  
   Many cities are reducing on-street parking. That can support demand for organised off-street hubs.

3. **Parking-led income**  
   Catalogue assets are parking-primary. Day-to-day operations sit with the operator under published terms.

4. **Additional services where contracted**  
   EV charging and related services may form part of a site where the agreement includes them. They should not be assumed to improve every ticket yield.

5. **Limits of the thesis**  
   A sound urban rationale does not remove investment risk. Targets may be missed. Capital may be lost.

**CTA:** View opportunities · Request access
